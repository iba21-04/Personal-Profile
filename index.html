About Me

Hi! I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student at Simon Fraser University with a passion for building innovative solutions through technology. I love blending hardware and software to create experiences that matter.

My journey as an engineering student has given me the chance to explore areas like software development, digital hardware, robotics, and embedded systems. I’m naturally curious and enjoy collaborating with others, whether it’s working on team projects, experimenting with new ideas, or solving challenging problems. I believe the best learning happens when you understand both the big picture and the small details along the way.

Outside of academics, I enjoy being involved in the Women in Engineering community, exploring new technologies, and baking in my free time. Baking gives me a creative break and reminds me that patience and experimentation often lead to the best results.
